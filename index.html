Privacy Policy — I Paid This

Last updated: 8 September 2026

I Paid This does not collect any data. There is no server to send it to.

This is the entire policy in one sentence, but the App Store requires the details, so here they are.

What we collect

Nothing. I Paid This has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. We never see your proof cards, serials, or what you share.

Where your data lives

Everything the app creates — unlocked tiers, serial numbers, purchase dates, and the Apple transaction IDs used to match a receipt to a StoreKit purchase — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Generated proof images live in a temporary folder on the device until you share or save them. This data is included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or to a computer); that backup is controlled entirely by you and by Apple, not by us. Deleting the app deletes the local data.

Network access

I Paid This has no server component and its own code makes no network requests of ours. Unlocked cards still work in airplane mode. The one exception is buying a tier: StoreKit talks to Apple to complete the purchase, and that exchange is between you and Apple.

Purchases

Each wealth tier is a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. After Apple confirms a purchase, the app stores the transaction identifier on your device so the matching proof card can be issued and so the same tier is not charged twice. Apple's own privacy policy covers the transaction itself.

Photos

If you tap Save, the app writes the proof images you just generated into your photo library. That is write-only and happens only when you ask. I Paid This does not read your camera roll, and it does not upload those images anywhere.

Sharing and clipboard

If you tap Share, iOS shows the system share sheet. The image and caption leave your device only if you pick a destination (Instagram, Threads, Messages, and so on). Those apps then follow their own privacy policies. The app also copies the caption onto your clipboard so you can paste it; that copy stays on the device.

Face ID / device passcode

After Apple confirms payment, the app may ask iOS to confirm the burn with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. That check runs entirely on your device through LocalAuthentication. We never receive biometric data.

Third-party code

There is none. I Paid This ships with no third-party SDKs and no external dependencies. StoreKit, SwiftData, LocalAuthentication, and the share sheet are Apple frameworks.

Children

I Paid This is not directed at children. It involves real-money In-App Purchases. We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if a verification server is added later — the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Since the app currently collects nothing, most changes would be wording fixes.

隱私權政策 — I Paid This

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 8 日

I Paid This 不收集任何資料。因為根本沒有伺服器可以送。

一句話就講完了，但 App Store 需要細節，所以以下逐項說明。

我們收集什麼

沒有。I Paid This 沒有分析工具、沒有當機回報、沒有廣告、沒有追蹤，也沒有帳號系統。我們看不到你的證明卡、序號，也不知道你分享到哪裡。

你的資料放在哪

App 產生的一切——已解鎖階級、序號、購買日期，以及用來對應 StoreKit 購買的 Apple 交易編號——只存在你裝置上 App 的私有儲存空間。產生出來的證明圖會暫存在裝置上的暫存資料夾，直到你分享或儲存。如果你有備份 iPhone（到 iCloud 或電腦），這些資料會包含在備份裡；那份備份完全由你和 Apple 控制，與我們無關。刪除 App 就會一併刪除這些本機資料。

網路連線

I Paid This 沒有後端伺服器，App 自己的程式碼也不會向我們發出任何網路請求。已解鎖的證明卡在飛航模式下一樣可用。唯一的例外是購買階級：StoreKit 需要連到 Apple 才能完成交易，那段往來是你和 Apple 之間的事。

購買

每一個財富階級都是一次買斷的非消耗型 App 內購買，完全由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。我們不會收到也不會儲存你的姓名、付款資訊或 Apple 帳號資料。Apple 確認購買後，App 會把交易編號存在你的裝置上，用來開立對應的證明卡，並避免同一階級被收第二次錢。交易本身適用 Apple 自己的隱私權政策。

相簿

你點「儲存」時，App 會把剛產生的證明圖寫進相簿。這是只寫不讀，而且只有你主動要求才會發生。I Paid This 不會讀取你的相機膠卷，也不會把那些圖片上傳到任何地方。

分享與剪貼簿

你點「分享」時，iOS 會叫出系統分享表單。圖片和文案只有在你選了目的地（Instagram、Threads、訊息等）之後才會離開裝置；那些 App 各自適用自己的隱私權政策。App 也會把文案複製到剪貼簿方便你貼上；那份複製只留在裝置上。

Face ID／裝置密碼

Apple 確認付款後，App 可能會請 iOS 用 Face ID、Touch ID 或裝置密碼確認這次燒掉。這項檢查完全在你的裝置上透過 LocalAuthentication 進行。我們不會收到任何生物辨識資料。

第三方程式碼

沒有。I Paid This 沒有內建任何第三方 SDK，也沒有外部相依套件。StoreKit、SwiftData、LocalAuthentication 與系統分享表單都是 Apple 的框架。

兒童

I Paid This 並非以兒童為對象，且涉及真實金錢的 App 內購買。我們不向任何人收集資料，兒童亦然。

政策變更

政策若有變更——例如之後若新增驗證伺服器——會更新這個網址上的版本並標註新日期。以目前 App 什麼都不收集來看，變更多半只會是文字修正。
