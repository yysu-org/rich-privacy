RICH — Support

Email easyyourlife777@gmail.com and you will get a reply. RICH is made by one person, so please allow a few days.

Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug. The app has no analytics, so a description of what you did is genuinely all we have to go on.

I paid but the tier is still locked

Force-quit and reopen RICH, and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Each tier is a one-time purchase tied to that account, not to the device. The app checks Apple's entitlements on launch and should unlock any tier you already own.

I deleted the app

Proof cards live only on your device. A reinstall can restore unlocked tiers from your Apple Account, but the original serial printed on a previous card is local — a new card may carry a new serial. There is no server copy we can recover.

Saving to Photos does nothing

iOS has to allow RICH to add photos. Check Settings → RICH → Photos, and allow adding to the library.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple. Request a refund with Report a Problem. We cannot process App Store refunds ourselves.

RICH — 支援

請寄信到 easyyourlife777@gmail.com，一定會回。這個 App 是一個人做的，回覆可能需要幾天。

回報問題時請附上裝置型號與 iOS 版本。App 沒有任何數據收集，所以你的描述就是我們唯一的線索。

我付了錢，但階級還是鎖著

請完全關閉再開一次 RICH，並確認登入的是當初購買的那個 Apple 帳號。每個階級都是一次買斷，綁定的是帳號，不是裝置。App 啟動時會向 Apple 核對權益，已購買的階級應該會自動解鎖。

我把 App 刪了

證明卡只存在你的裝置上。重新安裝後，已購買的階級可以從 Apple 帳號恢復，但先前那張卡上印的序號是本機產生的——新卡可能會有新序號。我們沒有伺服器備份可以幫你救。

儲存到相簿沒反應

iOS 必須允許 RICH 加入照片。到設定 → RICH → 照片，允許加入相簿。

退款
